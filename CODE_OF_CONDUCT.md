# Code of conduct

This project welcomes beginners and experienced FPGA engineers alike.
Contributors and maintainers must communicate respectfully, give actionable
technical feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
or disclosure of another person's private information.

Project spaces should remain focused on improving the hardware, software,
documentation, and learning experience. Maintainers may edit or remove
abusive, unsafe, misleading, or off-topic contributions and may restrict
participation after repeated violations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled in proportion to
their severity.
